FAQ

The questions clients ask before they start

Are you a digital marketing agency?

No. Zodiac Tech Soft is a software and IT services company — software development, websites, mobile apps, UI/UX, IT infrastructure, cloud and support. Digital marketing is one service among them, and we run it with the same engineering discipline as the rest. We are not a marketing-only agency.

What does it cost?

Competitive pricing for the quality delivered, quoted before the work starts. Fixed price for clear-scoped work; retainer or time-and-material when the scope has to move. Send your requirement and you get a transparent, itemised quote within 24 hours — no discovery-call gauntlet first, no obligation.

How long before we see anything?

Timelines we already commit to: a logo animation in 1–2 weeks, a corporate film in 3–6 weeks, a website in 3–5 weeks, a full web app or ERP in 2–4 months. Every proposal carries a schedule with weekly milestones, and you see a working demo at the end of each week rather than a status update.

Who owns what you build for us?

You do. Source code, design files, content, hosting credentials and documentation are handed over when the project ships. Nothing we build is designed to lock you into us — you can run it, extend it, or move it in-house at any point.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free post-launch support, and we keep running the systems we build. After that, an annual maintenance contract or retainer covers hosting, monitoring, security updates, backups and fixes — with a 24/7 emergency line for anything critical.

Why not hire a freelancer or a cheaper development shop?

For a small one-off, that can be the right call — we will tell you when it is. What you are buying from us is continuity: documented systems, a team that is still answering in year three, and one point of accountability when the website, the hosting, the software and the ads all touch each other.

Can you work within government tender and procurement processes?

We already do. Zilla Parishad, municipal corporations, Chandrapur Police, MAHAGENCO, the Forest Department, Gondwana University and the G20 Nagpur City programme are among our 24+ clients — so documentation, compliance, site surveys and multi-location rollout are routine work for us, not exceptions.

Can you handle both the software and the marketing?

Yes — that is the point of working with us. Software, infrastructure and production AND digital marketing happen under one roof, so the website, the tracking and the campaigns are built by the same team that has to answer for the enquiries they produce.

Do you take white-label work from other agencies?

Yes. A large share of our 172+ completed projects was white-label delivery for agencies and consultancies — software, apps, websites, film and 3D production under the partner's brand. We work to NDAs as standard, and your client relationship stays yours.

Do you work with clients outside Maharashtra?

Absolutely. We work with government institutions and private clients across India — Jharkhand, Gujarat, Pune, Nagpur, Mumbai, Bangalore — and internationally. Four offices (Chandrapur, Nagpur, Pune, Rajkot) make in-person meetings a short drive rather than a video call with strangers.

How do we start?

Send the form on the contact page, WhatsApp us, or call. You get a reply within 24 hours, then a short working call on your requirement, then a written proposal with scope, timeline and fixed price. If we are not the right fit, we will say so on that call.
