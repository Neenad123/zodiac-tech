FAQ

The questions clients ask before they start

Do you provide digital marketing?

Yes. We provide performance marketing, SEO, social media, content and conversion-focused campaigns. We can also connect marketing with websites, CRM and automation so leads don't get lost after they arrive.

How much does software development cost?

It depends on scope — a CRM module and a multi-tenant SaaS product are not the same build. Send the requirement and you get an itemised quote within 24 hours: fixed price where the scope is clear, retainer or time-and-material where it has to move.

How long does a website take?

A focused business website runs 3–5 weeks. Larger portals, e-commerce and multi-language builds take longer. Every proposal carries a schedule with weekly milestones, and you see a working demo each week.

Do you provide monthly support?

Yes — annual maintenance contracts and retainers covering hosting, monitoring, security updates, backups and fixes, plus ongoing development and campaign work. Every project also includes 30 days of free post-launch support.

Can you work with an existing system?

Yes. We integrate with what you already run rather than forcing a rebuild: legacy databases, ERP, payment gateways, WhatsApp and government portals. If replacing something is the better call, we will say so and show you why.

Do you provide white-label services?

Yes. A large share of our 172+ completed projects was white-label delivery for agencies and consultancies — software, apps, websites, film and 3D production under the partner's brand. We work to NDAs as standard.

Can you integrate CRM, WhatsApp and payment systems?

Yes. That combination is where most lead leakage gets fixed: enquiry captured in the CRM, acknowledged on WhatsApp in seconds, payment collected and reconciled without manual entry.

Do you provide Google and Meta Ads?

Yes — campaign structure, creative, landing pages and conversion tracking, run as one system. We report on enquiries and cost per enquiry, not impressions.

Can you build custom SaaS products?

Yes. Multi-tenant architecture, subscription billing, admin control and role-based access — built to be handed over, not rented from us.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with clients across India and internationally. Four offices — Chandrapur, Nagpur, Pune and Rajkot — make in-person reviews a short drive for most of central and western India.
