A software and IT services company — digital marketing is one of its services

Zodiac Tech Soft builds and runs software, websites, mobile apps, IT infrastructure and cloud for government institutions and private-sector organisations. Eight years of that work taught us where projects actually fail — in the plumbing, not the pitch: slow systems, unclear ownership, tracking nobody verifies.
