OBJECTIONS, HANDLED

Before you ask

Are you a digital marketing agency?

No. Zodiac Tech Soft is a software and IT services company — software development, websites, mobile apps, UI/UX, IT infrastructure, cloud and support. Digital marketing is one service among them, and we run it with the same engineering discipline as the rest. We are not a marketing-only agency.

What does it cost?

Competitive pricing for the quality delivered. Fixed-price for clear-scoped work; retainer or time-and-material for ongoing engagements. Send us your requirement and you get a transparent quote within 24 hours — no discovery-call gauntlet first.

How long before we see anything?

Timelines the studio already commits to: a logo animation takes 1–2 weeks, a corporate film 3–6 weeks, a full web app or ERP 2–4 months. Every proposal carries a detailed schedule and weekly milestones, and we stick to it.

Can you handle both the software and the marketing?

Yes — that is the point of working with us. Software development, IT infrastructure and production AND digital marketing happen under one roof, so the website, the tracking and the campaigns are built by the same team that has to answer for the numbers.

Do you take white-label work from other agencies?

Yes. A large share of our 172+ completed projects was white-label delivery for agencies and consultancies — software, apps, websites, film and 3D production under the partner's brand. We work to NDAs as standard.

Do you work with clients outside Maharashtra?

Absolutely. We work with government institutions and private clients across India — Jharkhand, Gujarat, Pune, Nagpur, Mumbai, Bangalore — and internationally. The four-city office network makes in-person meetings easy.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free post-launch support. After that, annual maintenance contracts and retainers cover hosting, monitoring, updates, campaigns and new development — most clients continue on one.

How do we start?

Send the form on the contact page or call. You get a reply within 24 hours, then a short working call on your requirement, then a written proposal with scope, timeline and price. No hard pitch, no obligation.
